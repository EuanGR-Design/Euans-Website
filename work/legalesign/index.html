Legalesign · 2021 – Present

Console

Legalesign is a secure, regulated e-signature platform used by governments, councils and large organisations. I rebuilt the whole legacy product as Console, feature by feature, and over five years shipped more than 100 features and workflows. Rather than tell every project the same way, each stage of how I work is shown through the project that shows it best.

My remit

From designer to product owner.

  1. 2021

    Joining, and the first Console release

    Joined Legalesign and started rebuilding the legacy product as Console, beginning with an MVP that matched the old product's core features.

    • MVP
    • UX
    • UI
  2. Two years

    Rebuilding the product, feature by feature

    Recreated the entire legacy product one epic at a time, while backend engineers rebuilt the platform to modern security standards. Every feature was researched, prototyped and tested with users before handover. I led the design team throughout.

    • Epics
    • User testing
    • Team lead
  3. Last 1–2 years

    Product ownership: design and delivery

    Without stepping away from design, I took on product owner duties, filling business needs and taking control of a larger part of the development process. I plan bi-weekly sprints in Jira, write the epics and tickets developers work from, and manage bugs and feature requests.
